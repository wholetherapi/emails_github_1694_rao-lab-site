Anika Rao, Tomasz Kaczmarek, Linh Pham

*Nature Methods 2025 (published).* FoldCast couples an equivariant message-passing backbone with a conditional flow-matching head to produce calibrated ensembles of protein backbone conformations. The method reports per-residue uncertainty estimates that correlate with experimental B-factors and improves RMSD on CASP-style benchmarks while keeping inference within a single GPU pass.
