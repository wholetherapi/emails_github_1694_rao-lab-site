---
title: "CellAnnotator: Weakly Supervised Single-Cell Type Annotation via Prototype Alignment"
venue: "RECOMB 2025"
status: "accepted"
---

Anika Rao, Mateo Silva, Rina Watanabe

*RECOMB 2025 (accepted).* CellAnnotator learns cell-type prototypes from marker-gene priors and aligns unlabeled single-cell embeddings to those prototypes with a contrastive objective. It transfers annotation across tissues and sequencing platforms without per-dataset fine-tuning, and provides calibrated confidence scores for downstream clustering.
