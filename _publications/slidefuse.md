Anika Rao, Pieter de Vries, Hana Mori

*MICCAI 2025 (published).* SlideFuse fuses tile-level whole-slide image embeddings with bulk transcriptomic profiles through a gated cross-attention module. The resulting representation improves molecular subtype classification on three public cohorts and remains robust when only a subset of transcriptomic panels is available.
