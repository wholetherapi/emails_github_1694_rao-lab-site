---
title: "ReguLattice: Scalable Gene Regulatory Network Inference from Perturbation Screens"
venue: "NeurIPS 2025"
status: "accepted"
---

Anika Rao, Samuel Osei, Ekaterina Volkova

*NeurIPS 2025 (accepted).* ReguLattice casts regulatory-network inference as sparse message passing over a learned lattice of gene modules. It scales to millions of perturbation observations and recovers known transcription-factor targets with higher precision than correlation-based baselines.
