title: "AnchorAlign: Fast Homology-Aware Sequence Alignment for Metagenomic Reads"
venue: "Bioinformatics 2025"
status: "published"
---

Anika Rao, Julia Berg, Wei-Lin Chen

*Bioinformatics 2025 (published).* AnchorAlign uses short conserved anchor k-mers to seed alignments before running a banded extension, giving homology-aware alignments of noisy metagenomic reads at a fraction of the cost of full dynamic programming. The implementation is distributed as part of the lab's read-processing pipeline.
